How can learning be a hobby?

A hobby counts as any activity that you do outside of your occupation for enjoyment. Learning is so much more than cramming for math or science classes that you might not be interested in. Learning happens every day as you get better at completing different tasks. Focusing on improving and learning in several areas is a fantastic hobby to have. You can learn about various topics, be it specific interests, creativity, social elements, or even physical activities.
